Privacy Policy for ArchBowlAR

Last Updated: Sept 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the application ArchBowlAR distributed via the Meta Horizon platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding data practices.

1. Data Collection & Platform Compliance

ArchBowlAR is a pure offline, single-player application designed to comply with the Meta Horizon OS Developer Data Use Policy. We do not collect, store, or log any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers from users. The application functions entirely locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is transmitted from the application to our servers or any third-party entities.

3. Spatial Awareness and Passthrough

ArchBowlAR utilizes the Meta Quest Passthrough feature to allow you to see your physical surroundings while playing. This feature is managed entirely by the Meta Horizon OS. ArchBowlAR does not access, record, store, or transmit your camera feed or any images of your physical environment.

4. Data Deletion

Since ArchBowlAR does not collect any user data, there is no personal data for us to store or delete.
