ArchBowlAR Beta

Your Portable Sports Arena.

🎮 Common

- Defaults to AR
- Simple VR mode, uses less power than the cameras.
- Currently there are 3 High Res backgrounds
- There are also 3 low res choices; black, aurora, storm.
- Model swaps, more are planned
- Practice mode is default.
- Scoring mode has a togglable scoreboard.
- Procedural arcade like sounds
- Offline play
- No signups or lobbies

Controls:

- Clicking left stick toggles scoreboard
- B/Y button to exit.
- A/X button to confirm
- menu button toggles the menu, navigate with thumbstick

🏹 Archery

- Target distances and speed options
- Most target movement modes are sitting friendly
- Modes like 180 and 360 (degrees) are best played standing
- Bow HUD, Arrow trails, Target finding arrow

Controls:

1 Squeeze grip for bow
2 Hold hands apart
3 Pull trigger for arrow
4 Bring hands together to nock arrow
5 Pull back and release trigger to fire
6 Releasing bow first cancels the shot

- After 3 arrows you switch to review mode.
- Clicking right stick toggles arrow paths
* You can only fire one arrow at a time, the arrow in flight has to hit something.

🎳 Bowling

- Natural standing play.
- Pin modes: 5 Vshape, 9 diamond, triangle; 10, 15, 21
- Wristwatch to show downed pins

Controls:

1 Simply grab the ball with the trigger.
2 Throw ball and release trigger

- A/X will reset pins in practice mode.
- In scoring pins reset on frames.
- Throwing or dropping the ball on ground does not count as a turn. Just pull trigger to pick it up again
- If the lane or gutter is hit by the ball it counts as a turn.

About ArchBowl

I made this because I wanted a bowling and archery game and I wanted to play outside.
At the time I didn't see anything like that made already, so I got started and here we are.
No online sign ups, just install and play!
Make sure to toss that bowling ball into the neighbours yard too..

ArchBowl was made by Colin (aka DerBen) using Godot, Blender and Gimp

Development assistance provided by AI collaboration.

This product was tested on humans!
